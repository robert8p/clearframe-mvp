Cogni

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026.

Plain-English summary. Cogni uses account, learning and subscription information to provide and improve the learning service. Cogni does not store payment-card details, sell personal data, or use third-party advertising trackers in the current mobile architecture.

Who operates Cogni

Cogni is operated by the developer identified on Cogni’s Apple App Store and Google Play listings (the “Cogni operator”). The final public-launch version of this policy should be reviewed with the operator’s legal identity and private support contact before broad commercial release.

Information Cogni processes

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers and account status.
  • Learning context: optional name and structured context such as audience, study/work context and learning goal.
  • Learning activity: question responses, confidence, response time, feedback, XP, streaks, skill scores, evidence/reliability and session history.
  • Service context: time zone and technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.
  • Subscription data: store, product, entitlement status, purchase/expiry timestamps, renewal/billing-state indicators and RevenueCat customer identifiers. Cogni does not receive or store full card details.

Why Cogni processes it

Cogni processes this information to authenticate users; provide personalised lessons and practice; calculate and display learning indicators; preserve progress across sessions; secure and rate-limit the service; provide customer support; operate subscriptions; restore access; understand product use; and meet legal, fraud-prevention and store obligations.

Learning indicators

Cogni’s Development Score is an adaptive learning indicator with separate evidence reliability. It is not a population percentile, validated IQ or cognitive-ability score, employment assessment, psychometric diagnosis or permanent grade.

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, Postgres database and server-side Edge Functions.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement and purchase-status infrastructure. RevenueCat receives the stable Cogni user identifier used to reconcile access across supported devices.
  • Apple / Google: app distribution and, when applicable, App Store or Google Play subscription billing. Their own privacy terms govern payment-account information they process.

Analytics

Current authoritative product analytics are stored in Cogni’s Supabase environment. Monetisation events record actions such as paywall views, purchase outcomes and restore outcomes without storing sensitive payment-card data. Percentages should be interpreted with their denominators and sample size.

Security and retention

Cogni uses platform-secure session storage, row-level database access controls, authenticated server APIs and server-side grading. Entitlement writes are server-owned. Data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Cogni, maintain security and satisfy legal or store obligations, subject to deletion rights and required transaction records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat.

Your choices and rights

You can update learning context in the app and delete your Cogni account from Profile. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Store subscriptions are managed separately from Cogni account deletion.

International processing

Cogni’s service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Appropriate provider contractual and legal safeguards should be confirmed as part of final legal review for the operator’s launch markets.

Children

Cogni is not intentionally designed in this release as a child-directed service. Store age-rating and any minimum-age requirement must be finalised before public launch.

Changes

This policy may change as Cogni’s service or legal obligations change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where required, giving additional notice.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, use the current contact method published on the Cogni Support page. A private support contact must be added before public paid launch.
