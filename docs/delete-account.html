Account controls

Delete your Cogni account

In the Cogni app:
  1. Sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the warning and confirm deletion.

What deletion removes

Cogni deletes the account and Cogni-side learning data associated with it, including profile context, responses, score history, streaks and the Cogni-side subscription-entitlement projection, subject to any records that must legally be retained by a separate store/payment provider.

What deletion does not do

Deleting Cogni does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. If you do not want a subscription to renew, cancel it separately in the store subscription settings before or after deleting the Cogni account.

Store transaction records

Apple, Google and RevenueCat may retain transaction or subscription records under their legal, security, fraud-prevention or financial-record obligations. Cogni does not claim to erase records that are controlled by those providers.

Can I undo deletion?

No. Cogni account deletion is intended to be permanent. If you later create a new Cogni account, previous learning history may not be recoverable.

For problems using in-app deletion, see Cogni Support.
