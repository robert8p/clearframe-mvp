Support

Subscription help

If Cogni Pro does not appear after purchase, make sure you are signed into the Cogni account you want to use, then open Profile → Restore purchases. If the store reports a purchase but server verification is still pending, keep Cogni online and retry Restore purchases.

Subscriptions are billed and cancelled through the store where you purchased them. On iPhone/iPad, use Apple’s subscription settings. On Android, use Google Play’s subscription settings.

Account and privacy

You can change your password, sign out or delete your Cogni account from Profile. See the account deletion page before deletion if you have an active store subscription.

Learning-data problems

If a lesson, score or session fails to load, first retry while online and then fully close/reopen Cogni. Do not repeatedly submit the same answer if the app says it was already recorded.

Contact

A private customer-support contact address must be added here and to both store listings before Cogni’s public paid launch. Closed testing may use the developer contact configured in the applicable store console. Do not post account, payment or other personal information in a public issue tracker.

Current support material for Cogni 0.4.0 store-readiness. Private support-contact setup remains a release gate.
