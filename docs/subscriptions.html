Cogni Pro

Subscription Terms

Effective 26 August 2026.

Cogni Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. The exact local price, billing period and any eligible introductory offer are shown by the Apple App Store or Google Play before you confirm a purchase. Cogni does not hardcode or independently charge your payment card.

Plans

Cogni intends to offer monthly and annual Cogni Pro subscriptions. The annual plan may be presented as better value when the actual store-returned prices support that comparison.

What Pro unlocks

Cogni Pro is designed to accelerate, not replace, the free learning habit. The initial release unlocks unlimited additional focused skill practice and full available progress-history trends (currently capped to one year in the mobile view). The free experience retains the starting check, current daily lesson/core session, basic skill map, basic progress snapshot, normal XP/streaks and account/privacy controls.

Billing and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple App Store or Google Play account when you confirm purchase. Unless you cancel, the subscription renews automatically at the store-displayed renewal price and period. Apple or Google may notify you of qualifying price changes under their rules.

Trials and introductory offers

Cogni only describes a trial or introductory offer in-app when the storefront makes an offer available and the app can responsibly determine eligibility. An offer shown to one user, platform or region may not be available to another. If a trial converts to paid service, the purchase sheet will show the applicable terms before confirmation.

Cancellation

You can cancel future renewal in your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation normally leaves paid access active until the end of the current paid period unless the store indicates otherwise. Deleting the Cogni account or uninstalling Cogni does not cancel a store subscription.

Restore and other devices

Use Restore purchases in Cogni when you have an existing store subscription that is not reflected in the app. Server-side premium access is verified against Cogni’s subscription entitlement state; a local app flag cannot grant protected premium API access.

Refunds, revocation and billing issues

Refunds are handled under Apple or Google policies. A refunded or revoked subscription does not retain Cogni Pro server access. Where a store reports an active grace period or billing-recovery period, Cogni may continue access through the verified entitlement expiry while the store resolves payment.

Support

See the Cogni Support page for subscription troubleshooting and store-management links.

Store purchase sheets control the actual price and offer presented to you. Formal legal review recommended before broad public paid launch.
