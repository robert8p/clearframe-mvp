Scientific boundary

Cogni's Development Score is an adaptive learning indicator with separate evidence reliability. It is not a population percentile, validated IQ or cognitive-ability score, employment assessment, psychometric diagnosis or permanent grade.

Subscription management

Cogni Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple App Store or Google Play. Deleting a Cogni account does not automatically cancel a store subscription. Follow the instructions on the account-deletion page to manage both separately.
