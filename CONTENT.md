# Content Method

Each challenge contains user-visible text and options; the answer key stores the correct option, explanation, thinking principle, AI-era application and likely error patterns. Objective grading is deterministic. AI-generated future content should follow Generate -> Validate -> Human Review -> Publish.

The seed bank contains 100 scenarios, including 30 AI-answer audits, 14 bias scenarios, 16 evidence-quality items, 12 causation items and realistic workplace/industry tagging. Treat it as pilot-quality seed content, not a scientifically validated assessment bank.
