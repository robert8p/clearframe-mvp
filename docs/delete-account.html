Account controls

Delete your Cogni account

In the Cogni app:
  1. Sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the warning and confirm deletion.

What deletion removes

When subscription services are configured, Cogni first requests permanent deletion of the RevenueCat Customer identified by your Cogni user ID. It then deletes the Cogni account and Cogni-side learning data associated with it, including profile context, responses, score history, streaks and the Cogni-side subscription-entitlement projection. If the external privacy deletion cannot be completed, the app leaves your Cogni account intact and asks you to retry rather than falsely reporting complete deletion.

What deletion does not do

Deleting Cogni or its RevenueCat Customer does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. If you do not want a subscription to renew, cancel it separately in the store subscription settings before or after deleting the Cogni account.

Store transaction records

Apple and Google control their own transaction, subscription, security, fraud-prevention and financial records. They may retain records where their rules or legal obligations require this. Cogni does not store your full payment-card information and does not claim to erase records controlled by the stores.

Can I undo deletion?

No. Cogni account deletion is intended to be permanent. If you later create a new Cogni account, previous learning history may not be recoverable. A still-active store subscription may require a store restore process and does not restore deleted Cogni learning history.

For problems using in-app deletion, see Cogni Support.
