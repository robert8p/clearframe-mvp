# Cogni artwork

The active app artwork is rendered natively from the Cogni orbital mark, gradients, horizon light, and abstract skill motifs in `components/visuals.tsx`.

`approved-dreamscape.png` and `approved-dreamscape.webp` are retired provenance assets only. They contain character portrait imagery and must not be required, bundled into rendered UI, or used as a background.

Keep release artwork aligned with the supplied Cogni boards: abstract orbital knowledge motifs, deep navy space surfaces, cyan/blue/violet progress light, and warm amber reserved for achievement or premium emphasis. Never introduce character artwork into backgrounds, onboarding, thumbnails, paywalls, or saved-topic surfaces.
